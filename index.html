Review

See what you are signing.

Coldly prepares every transaction for review first: sender, network, recipient, asset, amount and estimated fee. Its checks bring possible problems into view. They support your review; they do not promise every address or contract is safe.

  • Burn-address recipients
  • First-time recipients
  • Unlimited approvals
  • Certain suspicious contract interactions
  • Node chain ID matches the network you picked

iPhone

Your balances on your phone.

The Coldly iPhone app is a read-only window onto your wallet. It shows what you hold and cannot spend. Pair it once from Settings on your computer, then it reads the chain itself, even with your PC switched off.

  • No recovery phrase, keys or USB key on the phone
  • Face ID before balances show
  • Works with your computer off

Lost your USB?

Your recovery phrase rebuilds the wallet with a new USB and password. The USB is for everyday access; the phrase is what recovers your accounts, so keep it safe. You can revisit the setup guidance any time inside the app.

What Coldly is

The USB holds unlock material, not your coins. Your assets stay on their blockchains and signing happens on your computer. Coldly is a software wallet with a USB lock, not a hardware wallet. It does not turn a USB drive into secure signing hardware. It gives your wallet a physical step you can see and feel.
